# Privacy policy — `/privacy`

> Draft to be refined together. Describes the agency's privacy policy page.

## Purpose
Public page explaining how the site collects and uses personal data. Required by the GDPR business rule, and the
target of the **consent checkboxes** on the enquiry (LEAD-01) and contact (LEAD-02) forms.
Rendered in the public shell (`x-public-layout`).

## Access rules
- Public (no authentication).

## Content
- Who we are / data controller.
- What we collect (name, email, phone, message, consent).
- Why (respond to enquiries, suggest matching properties).
- Legal basis (consent).
- Retention.
- Your rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability).
- Cookies (session/security only).

## Notes
- Content lives in `resources/views/pages/privacy.blade.php` (static, English — localized later by LOC-01).
- Reached from the public footer ("Privacy policy") and from the consent labels in the two forms.
